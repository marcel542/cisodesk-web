Privacy

CISO Desk collects nothing. There is no account, no server of ours, and no analytics. This page explains what that means in practice.

Last updated 19 September 2026 · applies to version 0.1 and later

The short version. Everything you put into the app stays in one database on your iPhone. We cannot see it, we have no way to request it, and there is nothing to delete on our side because nothing ever arrives there.

What the app stores, and where

Your profile, the facts and rules you teach it, obligations, evidence, actions, findings, risks, decisions, documents you import, and anything waiting in the Inbox are held in a single database inside the app's own storage on your device. iOS encrypts it at rest. The app has no sync, no backup service of its own and no iCloud integration.

A small shared folder is used to hand items over from the share extension and from Shortcuts to the app. Those files are deleted as soon as the app has read them.

What the app can read, and only if you allow it

Calendar Read, to prepare meeting briefs and show what is coming. Events are read while the app is open and are not copied out of the calendar. Off until you switch it on.
Reminders Write only, and only for actions you explicitly accept. Off until you switch it on.
Files you share or import Read on device to extract text. Screenshots are read with on-device text recognition. The original file is not copied into the app.
Face ID or Touch ID Used only to unlock the app, if you turn that on. Biometric data never leaves the Secure Enclave and is never available to the app.

The language model

By default the app uses Apple's on-device foundation model. Text you give it is processed on the iPhone and does not leave it.

You can optionally allow Apple's Private Cloud Compute for long documents, which the on-device model cannot hold in one piece. That setting is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, the text of the document being analysed is sent to Apple's Private Cloud Compute, where Apple states the data is not retained and is not accessible to Apple. It is not sent to us, and we receive nothing either way.

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising or attribution libraries, and makes no network requests of its own.

What we collect

Nothing. No usage statistics, no crash reporting, no device identifiers, no contact details. The App Store privacy label for this app declares no data collection, and that is the whole story rather than a summary of it.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Settings → Privacy and data → Delete everything removes the database and its journal files, anything waiting from a share, any keychain items and every scheduled reminder. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of it as well. Because nothing is held anywhere else, there is no separate request to make.

Changes

If a future version does something materially different with your data, this page will say so and will carry a new date. Anything that would send data anywhere will be off by default and will ask first.
