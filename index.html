Know what matters. Know what you missed.

A chief of staff for a CISO, not another compliance database. It keeps track of the dates, notices what has slipped, and prepares what you need for the room you are walking into.

It runs entirely on your iPhone. One database on the device, no account, no server, no analytics. The language model runs on the device too.

What it does

  • Takes you through the day. One card, one decision, then the next — what is overdue, what is waiting to be reviewed, one question worth asking, the next step of your programme.
  • Watches the recurring duties that come with ISO 27001, NIS2, DORA and the CRA, and tells you when evidence has gone stale.
  • Reads what you hand it. Meeting notes, an audit report, a screenshot — it pulls out the actions, findings and decisions, and nothing is saved until you accept it.
  • Prepares your meetings. What is open, what needs a decision, what you are likely to be asked, what to bring.
  • Guides a new CISO through the first hundred days, built out of outcomes rather than tasks — so anything it already knows counts, and it never asks twice.

What it will not do

It will not give you a compliance verdict, state that something did not happen when it simply has no evidence, invent a date or a clause, or decide on your behalf that a piece of work is finished. Where it has nothing, it says so.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Reading prose and reviewing documents need Apple Intelligence; everything else works without it.
