Common questions

Why does it keep saying it does not know something?

By design. The app separates what it has evidence for, what it has assumed from something you said, and what it has simply not been told. An unknown is not a finding and is never shown as one. It fills in as you answer the occasional question it asks.

It will not tell me whether we are compliant.

Also by design. The app is not an auditor and cannot see your whole environment, so it reports what it found and what it could not find rather than a pass or a fail. It is a working tool, not advice, and it does not replace your auditor or your lawyer.

Some features say Apple Intelligence is unavailable.

Reading prose, reviewing a document and answering in sentences need Apple Intelligence, which requires a supported iPhone with the feature enabled in iOS Settings. Without it the app still tracks dates, finds dated commitments in text and works normally — it simply will not paraphrase.

How do I get things in quickly?

  • Share from any app — Outlook, Mail, Safari, Files, Photos — to CISO Desk.
  • Paste straight into the Inbox with the paste button.
  • Say “Add to CISO Desk” or “Remember this in CISO Desk” to Siri, or put either on the Action button.
  • PDFs in OneDrive or SharePoint can be picked through the Files browser.

Can it read my mailbox directly?

No. iOS gives no app access to Mail, and the app deliberately has no server and no cloud account of its own. Sharing a message takes one tap and keeps that boundary intact.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

On your iPhone only. Settings → Privacy and data → Delete everything removes all of it, including the database files. See the privacy page.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a security problem in the app, please write to marcel@lightspeedconsulting.ai with enough detail to reproduce it, and give us a reasonable period to fix it before disclosing it publicly.
